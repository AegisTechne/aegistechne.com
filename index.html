Services

Fractional CISO / vCISO

Ongoing security leadership on a monthly advisory retainer. I set direction, run the program, and represent security to your leadership, customers, and insurers.

Risk assessments & roadmaps

A clear picture of where you stand and a prioritized, realistic plan to improve, written for owners and executives, not just engineers.

Framework advisory

Readiness and program guidance across common frameworks: NIST CSF, NIST SP 800-53, NIST SP 800-171 / CMMC, SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, CIS Controls, the FTC Safeguards Rule, and NYDFS Part 500. Advisory only: I help you build and maintain the program; I don't perform certification assessments.

Safe AI adoption

Choose the right AI tools, set sensible boundaries for customer and business data, and automate the busywork so you get time back.
